PGCP Doc Verification

A document verification tool for admissions to the post-graduate certificate programmes in Advanced Computing, Big Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence.

Candidates upload their academic documents; admissions staff review them. Once a document has been accepted, the tool keeps an archive copy in a Google Drive account belonging to the admissions team.

The tool uses Google's drive.file permission only, which lets it see and manage the files it creates itself and nothing else in that Drive.
